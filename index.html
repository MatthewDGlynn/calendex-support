Common questions

  • Calendar and Reminders access are independent. Calindex remains usable when you connect only one.
  • Calindex changes an event or reminder only after you choose an action. Apple Calendar and Reminders remain the sources of truth.
  • Connecting Google directly lets Calindex list your calendars and display events from the calendars you select. Google access is read-only: Calindex does not create, change, or delete Google events.
  • A direct Google connection works in Calindex only. It does not add a Google account to Apple Calendar or copy Google events into Apple Calendar. To connect Google to Apple Calendar too, add the account in iOS Settings.
  • Google events refresh every minute while Calindex is active, when you return to the app, and when you refresh manually. You can disconnect Google in Calindex Settings.
  • Notification options can be changed in Calindex Settings or iOS Settings.
  • Import history is optional, stays on your device, and can be deleted in Settings.
