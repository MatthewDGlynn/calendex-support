Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026

Calendex is designed to work locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not sell, rent, or share personal information.

Calendar data

Calendex requests access to Apple Calendar so it can display upcoming events and add, edit, or delete events when you explicitly choose those actions. Calendar data is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Imported notes and history

Text you paste for event parsing is processed on your device. Optional import history is stored locally to help identify duplicates and changes. You can disable or delete import history in the app.

Notifications

If enabled, reminders and summaries are scheduled locally using Apple notification services. Calendex does not use a remote notification server.

Diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostics are disabled by default. If you enable them in Settings, technical error information may be sent to help improve app reliability. Calendar titles, event details, imported text, and personal notes are not intentionally included.

Tracking and advertising

Calendex does not use advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or third-party advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Calendex-managed settings and import history remain on your device until you remove them, delete history in the app, or uninstall the app. Events saved in Apple Calendar are controlled through Apple Calendar and your calendar provider.

Children

Calendex is a general-audience productivity app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Calendex changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
