Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

Calindex (formerly Calendex) is designed to work locally on your devices. It does not require an account and does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

Calendar data

Calindex requests access to Apple Calendar so it can display upcoming events and add, edit, or delete events when you explicitly choose those actions. Calendar data is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Reminder data

Apple Reminders access is optional and requested separately. If you connect it, Calindex can display reminders and create, edit, schedule, complete, or delete them only in response to your actions. Reminder data is processed on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Imported notes and history

Text you paste for event parsing is processed on your device. Optional import history is stored locally to help identify duplicates and changes. You can disable or delete import history in the app.

Notifications, widgets, Live Activities, and Apple Watch

If enabled, summaries and legacy event alerts are scheduled locally using Apple notification services. Widgets and Live Activities use local app data. The Watch companion receives a minimal agenda snapshot from the paired iPhone through Apple Watch Connectivity; notes and unnecessary private fields are not included. Calindex does not use a remote notification server.

External actions

When you choose Join, Directions, Call, Share, or Open in Calendar, Calindex passes the selected information to the relevant app or Apple system service. Calindex does not embed a mapping, geocoding, or meeting service.

Tracking and advertising

Calindex does not use analytics, diagnostics reporting, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or third-party advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Calindex-managed settings and import history remain on your device until you remove them, delete history in the app, or uninstall the app. Events and reminders saved through Calindex are controlled through Apple Calendar, Apple Reminders, and your configured account providers.

Children

Calindex is a general-audience productivity app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Calindex changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
